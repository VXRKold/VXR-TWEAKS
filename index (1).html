Quick answers

Frequently asked.

Will these tweaks increase my FPS?

Maybe, depending on your hardware and current settings. They are not guaranteed FPS boosts. GPU limits, CPU limits, thermals, and the game mode all matter.

How do I undo the power plan?

Open Windows Terminal or Command Prompt as administrator and run powercfg /setactive SCHEME_BALANCED. You can also choose Balanced in Windows Power settings.

Is this an official EM Tweaks website?

No. VXR Tweaks is its own project, using a dark-and-yellow gaming optimization style. It is not affiliated with EM Tweaks.

Should I disable antivirus for more FPS?

No. Keep Windows Security enabled. Disabling protection is not a sensible general-purpose gaming optimization.
